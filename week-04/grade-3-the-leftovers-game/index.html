The Leftovers Game

Grade 3 · Week 4 · Multiplication Explorers

13 cookies, 4 friends. Deal them onto the plates one round at a time. What do we do with the extra one?

13cookies
4friends

Each round puts one cookie on every plate. When there aren’t enough for a whole round, stop. What’s left over has a name: the remainder.

Class0
Teacher0

Rules: roll, share the counters in play into that many EQUAL groups, keep the leftovers as points. Counters in equal groups stay in play. The game ends when no counters remain, or after three turns in a row with no leftovers.

Teacher notes

Objective: decide odd or even by pairing, then generalize the last-digit rule. Flow: Partners? (warm-up with 7 friends) → Sort 1 to 10 (launch) → Test 1 to 20 with the egg carton (investigate) → Last digit on 14, 27, 38, 45, 100, 34 and 47 → Odd + Odd (extension: test five examples, then claim). Look for: do students trust the tested rule for new numbers like 34, 47 and 100, or keep re-pairing every time? Trusting a verified pattern is early generalization (MP8). When a student asks "is this right?", tap the check buttons together and ask "how could you check?"

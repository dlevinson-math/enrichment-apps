Teacher note on 7 brownies for 4 people

The lesson notes give 4 cuts for 7 brownies and 4 people (a whole each, then 3 brownies among 4). Three cuts is possible: cut 3 brownies into 34 and 14. Three people get a whole and a 34 piece; the fourth gets a whole and the three 14 pieces. Two cuts can't work (the app explains why). In general, the fewest cuts for b brownies among p people is p minus the greatest common factor of b and p. This app's answers were checked by computer search.

Difference means how far apart

Today's big question

Does every Diffy end in zeros?

Vote before you play. Tap to add a vote.

How to play

  1. Put 4 numbers on the corners of the square.
  2. On each side, find the difference of the two corners. Write it in the middle circle.
  3. Those 4 new numbers make the next square.
  4. Keep going until you get 0, 0, 0, 0. Count the rounds.

Let's try 8, 3, 5, 1 together. After each square, ask: Where is this heading?

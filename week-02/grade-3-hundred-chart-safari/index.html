Visual Classification Tag

Diagonal Steps

Multiples of 3 form neat diagonal staircases sloping across the hundred chart!

Why Does It Happen?

Because 3 is 1 less than 4 (or 10-7), every jump of +3 moves down 1 row (+10) and back 7 columns (-7), creating a crisp diagonal angle!

Digit Sum Secret: The sum of digits for any multiple of 3 is ALWAYS divisible by 3! (e.g., 2+7 = 9)

The "7s Knight's Move" Pattern

Discover why 7 creates a chess knight-like drift across rows and solve the Row 191-200 Challenge!

Knight Hop Logic: +10 Down, -3 Left = +7 Net Jump!

Why Does 7 Drift Like a Knight?

On a 10-wide chart, moving down 1 row adds +10. Since 7 is (10 - 3), taking a step of +7 is identical to jumping:

1. Down 1 Row (+10)
2. Left 3 Columns (-3)
Result: +7 Hop! (Chess Knight L-Shape)
Try It Yourself:

Start at 21. Move down 1 row (to 31), then move left 3 columns (to 28). You just added +7!

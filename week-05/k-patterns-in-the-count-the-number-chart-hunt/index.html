Kindergarten · Week 5 · Unit 1 checkpoint

Count on from 24. Listen for a bump!

Look for · Who slows down at 29, 39, 49? Tap any number on the tape to count from somewhere new.

Find every number that ends in 5.

Ends in:

Look for · Do students look at the last digit, or hunt one by one? Who notices the straight line?

Some numbers are hiding! Tap a blue card.

Look for · Do partners use the neighbors (before, after, above, below) to name it? The first set matches the worksheet: 13, 25, 29, 38, 42, 47.

What comes after?

Look for · Self-correction at the decade turn. “Fifty-ten” is the classic slip; the counter shows why it can’t be.

Count to 100 together. Stop at every bump!

The ones repeat.
The tens change.

Look for · Unit checkpoint: fluent counting with self-correction at the decade turns. Tap any number to light its column and its tens.

Past 100! Where are the bumps? Tap them.

Look for · Students who predict the 9s column before checking are using the pattern, not counting. Try after 100 and after 109 in “After 9” too.

What to look for

Unit checkpoint: students should factor systematically (test 1, 2, 3, … up to half the number, and use factor partners) and reason about factors in the abstract, for example “a prime’s only proper factor is 1, so it must be deficient.” When a student asks “is this right?”, ask “how could you check?”

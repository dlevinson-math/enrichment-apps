Talk about it

  • What stays the same when you rotate 3×6 to 6×3?
  • Is 3×6 a different rectangle from 2×9, or just a different arrangement?
  • A rectangle factory has exactly 24 tiles. Which whole-number side lengths are possible — and how could we prove we found all of them?

    Exit ticket

    List every factor pair of 28, then explain how you know your search could stop.

    28 = 1×28, 2×14, 4×7. Once your test width passes the point where width and height would swap places (around 5, since 5×5=25 and 6×6=36), any new pair is just a repeat you've already found in reverse — so 28 has exactly three rectangle shapes.
